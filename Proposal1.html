LANCER planning companion

This proposal explores an AI companion for planning LANCER tabletop campaigns and encounters.

LANCER is fairly intricate, and this AI companion would help streamline the planning process for GMs.

LANCER already has a online helper with Comp-Con.

I have found Comp-Con to be helpful more on the player side, and this AI companion would complement it by providing additional support for GMs.

The AI companion could assist with:

  • Generate encounters based on license level and party composition
  • Provide suggestions for mission objectives and story hooks
  • Offer advice on balancing encounters and pacing
  • Assist with NPC creation and development
