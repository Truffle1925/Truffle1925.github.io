D&D DM Helper

This proposal explores an AI helper for preparing and managing D&D sessions.

WOtC already has a significant amount of content and tools available for DMs, this aims to build upon that foundation.

It would primarially focus on automating routine tasks and providing intelligent suggestions to enhance the gameplay experience.

The AI helper could assist with:

  • Generate encounters based on party level and party composition
  • Provide suggestions for enemy behavior and tactics
  • Offer advice on balancing encounters and pacing
  • Assist with NPC creation and development
