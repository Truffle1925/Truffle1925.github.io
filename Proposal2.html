Worldbuilding Checklist Assistant

This proposal explores an AI assistant for organizing and completing worldbuilding tasks.

Worldbuilding can be a complex and time-consuming process, and this AI assistant would help streamline it.

By researching and analyzing worldbuilding elements, the AI assistant could provide structured guidance and reminders for completing various aspects of the worldbuilding process.

The AI assistant could assist with:

  • Following guidelines for developing world elements
  • Provide suggestions for developing lore and history
  • Offer advice on creating interesting and immersive worlds
  • Assist with nation building and political systems
