ABOUT NORTHSTAR

A sense of direction.
A spirit of discovery.

Northstar Studios is an independent game studio making games and interactive experiences guided by exploration, discovery, and play.

Our background is in programming and interactive media. Our focus is what those tools make possible: responsive gameplay, worlds worth exploring, and the small details that give an experience its character.

We’re currently building Thunderbird. You can also play Goblin Survivors, a top-down wave-based shooter, on itch.io.
